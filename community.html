Backflow Compliance, Built by a Nassau County Tester

Potaflow is a DOH-1013 filing tool created by a licensed Nassau County backflow tester who got tired of paper forms and clunky workflows. It lets licensed testers fill out and submit their backflow test reports directly from their phone — right at the job site.

No paper. No per-test fees. No chasing down reports later. Just a straightforward digital tool designed by someone who does the work every day.

Built for Nassau County & New York testers
